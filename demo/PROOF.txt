OddSockets .NET SDK - live two-client regression proof
=======================================================

Captured against the LIVE OddSockets platform (worker w002-oddsockets-1)
using the SAME SDK a consumer installs. Two independent client connections
(alice = subscriber, bob = publisher); anything alice receives can ONLY have
travelled through the OddSockets worker - it is not a local echo. No mocks.

Build:  docker build -f demo/Dockerfile -t oddsockets-csharp-demo .
Run:    docker run --rm -e ODDSOCKETS_API_KEY="ak_..." oddsockets-csharp-demo

--- What this proves ----------------------------------------------------

Scenario 1 (core pub/sub): bob publishes on his own connection; alice
receives the message with a matching nonce on her separate connection, then
reads live presence and unsubscribes - a genuine end-to-end round-trip.

Scenario 2 (enhanced events): bob fires enhanced.StartTyping and
enhanced.AddReaction; alice receives "user_typing" and "reaction_added" on
her PUBLIC raw event surface (client.On) - proving the enhanced surface is
wired to the real Socket.IO transport and broadcasts traverse the worker,
not a local simulation.
